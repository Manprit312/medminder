Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

Last updated: April 2026

shield

MedMinder is built with privacy first.

We store only what's needed to run the app. We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and never use it for any purpose beyond providing MedMinder to you.

What we collect

account_circle

Account information

Your Google email address, used to identify your account. No passwords are stored — sign-in is handled entirely by Google.

medication

Medication data

Medication names, dosage notes, reminder times, and dose logs (taken / skipped / missed) that you enter manually.

people

Profile information

Names of profiles you create (e.g. "Dad", "Self"). No date of birth, address, or government ID is collected.

smartphone

Device data

Push notification tokens (to deliver dose reminders on your device). These are stored locally and never shared.

How we use it

notifications_active

Dose reminders

Scheduled reminders are sent locally on your device. No notification content is sent to external servers.

volunteer_activism

Caretaker sharing

When you send a caretaker invite, the invite link and the invited person's WhatsApp number are stored temporarily to facilitate the connection. The phone number is deleted once the invite is accepted or expires.

payments

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. MedMinder never sees or stores your card number, UPI PIN, or banking credentials. We only record whether your purchase was successful.

Who we share it with

No one, except as strictly necessary:

  • check_circle Google Firebase — authenticates your sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • check_circle Razorpay — processes payments for MedMinder Plus. Governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy.
  • check_circle Render.com — hosts the backend server in a secure data centre.

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics platforms, or data brokers.

Your rights

delete_forever

Delete your data

Sign out and email us to request full account deletion. All your profiles, medications, and dose logs will be permanently removed within 30 days.

download

Export your data

You can request a copy of all data we hold about you. Email us and we'll send it within 7 days.

edit_note

Correct your data

Profile names and medication details can be edited directly in the app at any time.

mail

Questions or requests?

Contact us at privacy@medminder.app — we aim to respond within 48 hours.

MedMinder is a reminder tool only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician or pharmacist before changing any medication.

This policy may be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
