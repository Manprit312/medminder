MedMinder Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

MedMinder is built with privacy first. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

📅 Last updated: April 2026

On this page

1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. Who we share with 4. Data retention 5. Your rights 6. Security Contact

Short version: We store only what's needed to run MedMinder — your email (via Google), your medication data you enter, and payment confirmation. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. You can export or delete everything at any time from the app.

1. What we collect

✉️

Account information

Your Google email address, used to identify your account. No passwords are stored — sign-in is handled entirely by Google Sign-In.

💊

Medication data

Medication names, dosage notes, reminder times, and dose logs (taken / skipped / missed) that you enter manually in the app.

👨‍👩‍👧

Profile information

Names of profiles you create (e.g. "Dad", "Self"). No date of birth, address, or government ID is ever collected.

📱

Device data

Push notification tokens to deliver dose reminders on your device. These are stored locally and never shared with third parties.

2. How we use it

🔔

Dose reminders

Scheduled reminders are generated locally on your device. No notification content is sent to external servers.

🤝

Caretaker sharing

When you send a caretaker invite, the invite link and the recipient's WhatsApp number are stored temporarily. The phone number is deleted once the invite is accepted or expires (7 days).

💳

Payments

Payments for MedMinder Plus are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN, or banking credentials. We only record whether your purchase was successful.

3. Who we share it with

No one, except as strictly necessary to operate the service:

  • ✓Google Firebase — authenticates your sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • ✓Razorpay — processes payments. Governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy.
  • ✓Render.com — hosts the backend server in a secure data centre.

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics platforms, data brokers, or any other third parties.

4. Data retention

⏳

Active accounts

Your data is retained for as long as you have an active MedMinder account.

🗑️

Account deletion

When you delete your account (from Settings → Your data → Delete account), all your data is permanently removed from our servers immediately.

🔑

Invite tokens

Caretaker invite links expire automatically after 7 days if not accepted and are then deleted.

5. Your rights

📥

Export your data

In the app: Settings → Your data → Download my data. This downloads a complete JSON file of all your profiles, medications, and dose history instantly.

🗑️

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data immediately.

✏️

Correct your data

Profile names and medication details can be edited directly in the app at any time.

🚫

Withdraw consent

You may stop using MedMinder and request account deletion at any time. No penalty or fee applies.

6. Security

🔒

Encryption in transit

All data between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

🏠

Authentication

Accounts are protected by Google Sign-In. We never store passwords. Session tokens are signed with a secret key and expire after 30 days.

Questions or requests?

Contact our privacy team at privacy@medminder.app. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

MedMinder is a medication reminder tool only. It does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician or pharmacist before changing any medication regimen.

This policy applies to the MedMinder mobile app and associated backend service. It may be updated periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
